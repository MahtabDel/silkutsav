Shipping & Delivery Policy

Bringing Bhagalpuri silk to your doorstep

← Back to SilkUtsav
Effective date: 22 November 2026

Welcome to SilkUtsav! We are delighted to bring the elegance of Bhagalpuri silk sarees and suits to your doorstep. We aim to deliver your products safely and on time.

1. Order processing time

  • Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days after successful order confirmation.
  • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays may be processed on the next working day.
  • During festivals, promotional sales or high-demand periods, processing may take additional time.

2. Estimated delivery timeline

Within India, delivery generally takes 4–8 business days after dispatch. Remote locations and certain serviceable areas may require additional time. These are estimates and may vary depending on the courier partner, destination and circumstances outside our control.

3. Shipping charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before the order is confirmed. Free-shipping offers will be communicated on the website or during promotional campaigns. Any applicable additional charges will be disclosed before order confirmation.

4. Order tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, we will share shipment tracking details through SMS, WhatsApp or email, wherever available.

5. Delivery address

Please ensure that your delivery address, PIN code and contact number are correct when placing your order. Incorrect or incomplete address details may cause delivery delays or failed delivery attempts.

6. Delayed or undelivered orders

If your order is delayed or marked as delivered but you have not received it, contact our support team. We will coordinate with our courier partner to investigate and assist you.

7. Damaged or tampered packages

If your package arrives visibly damaged or tampered with, photograph the package before opening it, record an unboxing video where possible, and contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Share your order number and supporting photographs or video so our team can investigate.

8. Cash on Delivery

If Cash on Delivery is available, you can pay the applicable order amount at the time of delivery. COD availability may depend on your delivery location and courier service.
