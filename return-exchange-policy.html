Return & Exchange Policy

Fair and simple support for eligible SilkUtsav orders

← Back to SilkUtsav
Effective date: 22 November 2026

At SilkUtsav, we take pride in bringing you beautiful Bhagalpuri silk sarees and suits. We carefully inspect products before dispatch so that you receive the quality you deserve.

1. Return eligibility

You may request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your order, subject to the conditions below:

  • The product must be unused, unworn and unwashed.
  • Original tags, labels and packaging must be intact.
  • The product must not have stains, damage, perfume marks or signs of use.
  • The product must be returned with all original accessories, where applicable.

2. Damaged, defective or incorrect products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, an item different from the one ordered, or a product with missing components, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Share your order number and clear photographs; an unboxing video is helpful where available.

After verification, we will arrange an appropriate replacement, exchange or refund. SilkUtsav will bear return shipping costs for verified damaged, defective or incorrect items.

3. Products not eligible for return

  • Products that have been used, washed or altered.
  • Products damaged due to improper handling after delivery.
  • Products returned without original tags or packaging.
  • Products showing signs of wear, stains or perfume marks.

4. How to request a return

  1. Contact us by email or WhatsApp within the applicable time window.
  2. Share your order number and reason for return.
  3. Attach clear photographs or videos of the product.
  4. Wait for our team to review the request and provide return instructions.
  5. Pack the product securely and send it using the approved return method.
Please note: Do not ship any product back without receiving return instructions from our team.

5. Exchanges

Exchange requests are subject to product availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, you may choose an eligible refund.

6. Important note about silk and handloom products

Bhagalpuri silk and handloom products may have slight variations in colour, texture, weave and design due to their handcrafted nature. Minor variations inherent to handloom craftsmanship are not, by themselves, considered manufacturing defects. Actual colours may also vary slightly depending on your device screen settings.
