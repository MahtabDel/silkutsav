Refund Policy

Clear information about refunds for eligible orders

← Back to SilkUtsav
Effective date: 22 November 2026

At SilkUtsav, customer satisfaction is important to us. We aim to provide a transparent and hassle-free refund process for eligible orders.

1. Refund eligibility

Refunds may be issued in the following situations:

  • An eligible return has been approved.
  • The product received is verified as damaged or defective.
  • An incorrect product has been delivered.
  • An order is cancelled before dispatch.
  • An order cannot be fulfilled because the product is unavailable.

All refund requests are reviewed under our Return & Exchange Policy.

2. Refund processing time

Once a returned product has been received and inspected, we will notify you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds will generally be initiated within 5–7 business days after approval. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account may depend on your bank or payment provider.

3. Refund method

  • Prepaid orders: Refunds will generally be credited to the original payment source wherever supported.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: Approved refunds will be transferred through a verified bank account or another mutually agreed payment method. We may ask for the necessary bank details.

4. Shipping charges

Original shipping charges, if any, will be refunded when the return is due to a verified defective, damaged or incorrect product, or another circumstance where a shipping refund is applicable. For other approved returns, shipping charges may be non-refundable where legally permissible and clearly disclosed before purchase.

5. Order cancellation

You may request cancellation before the order is dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. Please contact our support team for assistance under the applicable return policy.

6. Delayed refunds

If you have not received your refund within the expected timeframe:

  1. Check your bank account or payment statement.
  2. Contact your bank or payment provider.
  3. Contact SilkUtsav with your order number and refund reference, if available.
